## 中文

**一句话。** 这本书不预测崩溃，而是诊断"系统是否来得及自救"；它的核心变量是
**时间错配 τ = T_min / T_available** 与 **可控性 C = T_policy / T_threat = 1/τ**。

现代社会的脆弱性，除了结构来源，还有一个独立的时间来源：能加速的子系统
（金融、信息）恰是驱动冲击的一方，不能加速的子系统（政治、心理、生态）恰是
必须回应的一方。当多个子系统的时间尺度无法对齐，某些系统状态在数学上就不存在
及时的控制方案——此时理性的选择不是"更快更用力"，而是**预先减小暴露**，并为
不可避免的失效预设"优雅降级"的顺序。

**本版最重要的三项推进：**

1. **从诊断到行动：可自检的流程。** 第 11 章新增"操作检验流程"——五步自检
   （界定冲击 → 时间 → 空间 → 变量 → 主导维度）与三档判定（可描述 / 条件可描述 /
   不可描述）。它回答了一个实践者最实际的问题：*"这个问题还在我的局部可描述范围之内吗？"*
2. **学习滞后：二阶时间错配。** 第 13 章提出 ρ_learn = T_learning / T_threat，
   指出经验积累本身也有时间约束——当新失效模式的涌现快于"检测→理解→制度化调整"
   的速度，经验便不再累积为能力。**并明确：框架不从中心解决它，而是把可测的尺子交给
   各领域的真诚实践者。**
3. **后记：方法论的自限性与伦理立场。** 说明框架为何不替医疗、气候、金融、教育给出
   具体方案（这是逻辑必然，而非谦虚）；为什么"全知全能"的姿态会带来灾难；以及这本书
   想做的事与其说是"说服"，不如说是**解放**——让各领域的实践者有勇气诚实地谈论
   自己面对的时间错配。

**适合谁读。** 政策制定者、风险与危机管理者、系统科学与社会科学的读者，以及任何
需要在"来不及"的世界里做真实判断的人。它提供的不是预言，而是一把尺子和一套可反驳的
登记机制。

## English

**One line.** This book does not forecast collapse; it diagnoses whether a system
*still has time to save itself*. Its core variables are the **temporal mismatch
τ = T_min / T_available** and **controllability C = T_policy / T_threat = 1/τ**.

Beyond structural fragility, modern societies carry an *independent temporal* source of
vulnerability: the subsystems that can accelerate (finance, information) are exactly the
ones driving shocks, while the subsystems that cannot accelerate (politics, psychology,
ecology) are exactly the ones that must respond. When their time scales cannot be aligned,
some system states admit **no timely control in principle** — and the rational move is not
to try harder but to **reduce exposure in advance** and predefine a "graceful degradation"
order for unavoidable failures.

**Three advances in this edition:**

1. **From diagnosis to action: a self-checkable procedure.** Chapter 11 adds a five-step
   local-describability test and a three-tier verdict (describable / conditionally
   describable / not describable) — answering the practitioner's concrete question:
   *"Is this problem still within my describable range?"*
2. **Learning lag: a second-order mismatch.** Chapter 13 introduces
   ρ_learn = T_learning / T_threat, showing that experience accumulation is itself
   time-constrained. The framework deliberately does **not** solve this centrally; it hands
   a measurable ruler to sincere practitioners in each domain.
3. **A postscript on methodological self-limitation and ethics** — why the framework
   refuses to prescribe domain-specific solutions (a logical necessity, not modesty),
   why the posture of omniscience breeds disaster, and why the book aims less to
   *persuade* than to **liberate**: to let practitioners name their temporal mismatch
   honestly.

**Who it is for.** Policymakers, risk and crisis managers, and readers in systems and
social science who must make honest judgments in a world of "too late". It offers not
prophecy but a ruler and a falsifiable register.
